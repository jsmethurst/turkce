How Tekrar works

Tekrar uses spaced repetition to put your weakest words in front of you most often.

Each word's frequency is derived from your last 25 answers: a long run of right answers pushes it further away (up to 30 days), a miss pulls it back closer, and words you often get wrong keep coming back.
